01 / POLITE REQUESTS

Ask clearly and politely.

I'd like one adult ticket, please.Saya ingin satu tiket dewasa.
Could you tell me where the Ferris wheel is?Bisakah Anda memberi tahu letak bianglala?
How much is an adult ticket?Berapa harga satu tiket dewasa?
How long is the queue?Berapa lama waktu antreannya?

I'd like = I would like, bentuk sopan untuk menyatakan keinginan. Setelah Could you tell me where…, gunakan urutan pernyataan: where the Ferris wheel is, bukan where is the Ferris wheel.

02 / DIRECTIONS

Find your way around.

Go straight along the main path.Jalan lurus mengikuti jalur utama.
Turn left at the café.Belok kiri di kafe.
The carousel is west of the main path.Komidi putar berada di sebelah barat jalur utama.
The ticket booth is near the entrance.Loket tiket berada dekat pintu masuk.

Near = dekat; next to = tepat di sebelah; opposite = berseberangan. Arah kiri/kanan bergantung pada arah hadap. Sebutkan titik awal jika memberi petunjuk perjalanan.

03 / PREFERENCES & PLANS

Choose. Sequence. Explain.

I'd rather ride the Ferris wheel.Would rather diikuti bentuk dasar kata kerja: ride.
I'm going to try the water ride.Be going to + verb untuk rencana yang sudah diputuskan.
First, we'll ride the carousel. Then, we'll visit the café.First, next, then, finally menunjukkan urutan.
Let's choose the carousel because the queue is shorter.Because menghubungkan pilihan dengan alasan.
04 / COMPARISONS & SAFETY

Make a sensible choice.

The roller coaster is more exciting than the carousel.More + adjective + than untuk kata sifat seperti exciting.
The carousel queue is shorter than the coaster queue.Shorter adalah bentuk komparatif dari short.
Please keep your seat belt fastened.Harap tetap memakai sabuk pengaman.
You must follow the ride attendant's instructions.Must menyatakan kewajiban.

Latihan bahasa bukan petunjuk keselamatan lengkap. Saat berkunjung, ikuti aturan resmi dan arahan petugas wahana.

You're up. What would you say?

Pilih respons sesuai konteks. Respons kurang tepat mendapat penjelasan dan bisa dicoba lagi.
